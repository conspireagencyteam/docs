## Email types

| Email | When it's sent |
|-------|----------------|
| **Subscription activation** | When a customer first subscribes |
| **Upcoming charge** | Before a recurring charge (configurable lead time) |
| **Charge confirmation** | After a successful recurring payment |
| **Subscription cancelled** | When a subscription is cancelled |
| **Card declined** | When a payment fails |
| **Card expiring** | When a saved card is about to expire |

## Email settings

In the Bonde app, open **Subscriptions** and click **Email settings** under Email notifications. The page has three cards:

- **Domain authentication**: add and verify your sending domain (see [Domain verification](/docs/emails/domain-setup)).
- **Sender information**: once a domain is verified, set the **Sender name** shown in customer inboxes and the sender address (**Email prefix** @ **Domain**).
- **Additional settings**:
  - **Store owner email**: the address that receives copies of customer notifications.
  - **BCC storeowner on all customer notifications**: receive a hidden copy of every customer email.
  - **CC storeowner on all customer notifications**: add the store owner as a visible recipient.
  - **Disable SMS passcode authentication**: customers are offered an email passcode only, not SMS.

Click **Save changes** at the top of the page.

### Get a copy of every customer email

1. In **Additional settings**, enter your address in **Store owner email**.
2. Check **BCC storeowner on all customer notifications**.
3. Click **Save changes**.
