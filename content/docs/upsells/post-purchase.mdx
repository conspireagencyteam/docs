---
title: "Post-purchase upsells"
description: "Configure one-click upsells that appear right after checkout."
---

Post-purchase upsells appear immediately after a customer completes checkout, before the order confirmation page. Customers can add products to their order with a single click without re-entering payment information.

## How it works

1. Customer completes checkout and payment
2. Instead of going directly to the thank you page, they see your upsell offer
3. Customer can accept (one-click add to order) or decline
4. If accepted, the order is updated with the upsell product
5. Customer proceeds to thank you page

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Bonde for the post-purchase page">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings > Checkout** and scroll to **Post-purchase page**.

    Select **Bonde Subscriptions & Bundles**, then click **Save**. Shopify shows one app on the post-purchase page at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a post-purchase upsell">
    In the Bonde app, go to **Upsells** and click **Create Upsell**.

    Select **Post-Purchase** as the upsell type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure trigger conditions">
    Set when this upsell should appear:
    - **Product in Order**: Show when specific products were purchased
    - **Order Value (Minimum)**: Show for orders over a certain amount
    - **Order Value (Maximum)**: Show for orders under a certain amount

    Multiple conditions use AND logic: all must be met for the upsell to show. An upsell with no conditions shows after every order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the products to offer">
    Click **Add Products** and choose one product or several.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a discount (optional)">
    Configure a discount for the upsell:
    - **Percentage off**: e.g., 20% off
    - **Fixed amount off**: e.g., $10 off
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set priority">
    The page shows one upsell per order. If several match, the one with the highest priority shows. To offer two products on the same page, add both to one upsell.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the page looks like

The page follows your checkout's branding (colors, fonts and logo), and its layout depends on how many products the upsell offers:

- **One product**: a large product image beside the title, price, order totals and the **Pay now** button.
- **Two or more products**: a grid of products, up to three per row. The customer clicks **Add** on each product they want, then pays for the selection in one charge.

On a phone, both layouts stack into a single column.

## Page settings

These settings are on the upsell's edit page:

- **Offer expires after (minutes)**: shows a countdown and closes the page when it runs out. Leave blank for no time limit.
- **Shipping fee**: charged once for everything the customer adds. Leave blank for free shipping.
- **Page headline**: the line at the top of the page. Leave blank to use "Add these to your order", or "Add the (product name) to your order" for a single product.
- **Line under the headline**: leave blank to use "Ships with your order. One click, no need to re-enter payment." Write your own if your products do not ship, for example digital downloads.
- **Let shoppers choose a quantity**: shows a quantity picker (1 to 5) on each product. Turn it off to add each product once.

Change a setting, then click **Save**.

## Troubleshooting

**Upsell not showing?**
- Verify Bonde is selected under **Settings > Checkout > Post-purchase page**
- Check that trigger conditions match the test order
- Ensure the upsell is set to "Active"
- If another post-purchase upsell also matches, the one with the higher priority shows instead
- Test on a real checkout (not preview mode)

**Customer can't accept upsell?**
- Check Shopify admin for payment errors
- Ensure the upsell product is in stock
