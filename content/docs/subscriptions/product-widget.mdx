title: "Product page widget"

---

The subscription widget displays on your product detail pages, allowing customers to choose between one-time purchase and subscription options. By default, the widget only shows for products that have subscription plans configured.

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme customizer">
    Navigate to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your product template">
    Select the product template where you want to add the subscription widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    Add the **Bonde: Subscription PDP** app block to your product form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to publish your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Layouts

Click the app block in the theme customizer and choose a **Layout**:

- **Classic**: one-time purchase first, then Subscribe & save with a frequency dropdown and a short benefits line. Existing stores keep this layout until you switch.
- **Optimized**: the conversion-focused box. Subscribe & save comes first and is pre-selected, with a corner ribbon, the one-time price struck through, a benefit checklist, and one button per frequency showing its saving. One-time purchase sits underneath.

### Optimized layout settings

- **Pre-select Subscribe & save**: start with the subscription chosen.
- **Corner ribbon**: your text; `{discount}` becomes the best plan discount, for example 15%. Leave it empty to hide the ribbon.
- **Show the one-time price struck through**.
- **Benefits**: one line each; `{discount}` becomes the selected plan's discount.
- **Learn more link**: text and URL, hidden when the URL is empty.
- **Frequency label**: the heading above the frequency buttons.
- **Quantity and add to cart**: most themes already show these under the block, so leave it on the theme's controls. Turn on the stepper, or the stepper and a button, only if your theme does not.
- **Button text**: `{price}` becomes the price times the quantity.

The customizer preview shows the layout with sample plans, so you can see your edits as you make them.

## Colors

Set the text, background, accent and border colors and the corner radius to match your theme.

## How it works

When a product has subscription plans configured:

1. The widget displays one-time purchase and subscription options
2. Customers choose a frequency, from a dropdown in Classic or buttons in Optimized
3. Each option shows its discount, and free shipping where the plan has it
4. When they add to cart, the subscription plan is attached to the cart item

Prepaid plans show the price per delivery and the total per charge; see [Prepaid and commitment plans](/docs/subscriptions/prepaid-plans).
