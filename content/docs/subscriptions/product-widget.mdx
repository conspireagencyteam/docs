The subscription widget displays on your product detail pages, allowing customers to choose between one-time purchase and subscription options. By default, the widget only shows for products that have subscription plans configured.

## Add the subscription widget to your product page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme customizer">
    Navigate to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to your product template">
    Select the product template where you want to add the subscription widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app block">
    Click **Add block** in the product information section, open the **Apps** tab and choose **Subscription Widget** (Bonde Subscriptions & Bundles). Place it above the Buy buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to publish your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customize the subscription widget

Select the **Subscription Widget** block in the theme customizer to change its layout, text, colors and fonts.

### Layouts

The block's **Layout** setting is **Auto** by default: it follows the layout chosen in the Bonde app under **Subscriptions > Settings > Subscribe box layout**. Stores that installed Bonde before October 2026 are set to Classic there, so nothing changed on their product pages; a prompt on the Subscriptions page lets you switch to Optimized with one click, without touching the theme. New stores start on Optimized.

You can also pin a block to a layout in the theme customizer:

- **Classic**: one-time purchase first, then Subscribe & save with a frequency dropdown and a short benefits line.
- **Optimized**: the conversion-focused box. Subscribe & save comes first and is pre-selected, with a corner ribbon, the one-time price struck through, a benefit checklist, the journey timeline, and one button per frequency showing its saving. One-time purchase sits underneath.

Both layouts show a plan's [introductory offer](/docs/subscriptions/configuration#introductory-offer) as the first-order price with the ongoing price after it.

#### Optimized layout settings

- **Pre-select Subscribe & save**: start with the subscription chosen.
- **Corner ribbon**: your text; `{discount}` becomes the best plan discount, for example 15%. Leave it empty to hide the ribbon.
- **Show the one-time price struck through**.
- **Benefits**: one line each; `{discount}` becomes the selected plan's discount.
- **Learn more link**: text and URL, hidden when the URL is empty.
- **Show the journey timeline** and its title: the "What you get" list under the benefits, built from the plan's introductory offer, your [subscription journey](/docs/subscriptions/journeys) and milestone rewards. It only appears when something changes from order to order.
- **Frequency label**: the heading above the frequency buttons.
- **Quantity and add to cart**: most themes already show these under the block, so leave it on the theme's controls. Turn on the stepper, or the stepper and a button, only if your theme does not.
- **Button text**: `{price}` becomes the price times the quantity.

The customizer preview shows the layout with sample plans, so you can see your edits as you make them.

#### Different text per product

Text settings with the dynamic source icon next to them, such as **Corner ribbon** and the button text, can be connected to a product metafield, including a metafield that points to a metaobject. Click the icon and choose the metafield, and each product page shows its own text instead of one value for every product.

### Colors

Set the text, background, accent and border colors and the corner radius to match your theme.

### Typography

**Typography** is **Bonde default** unless you change it. Choose **Theme fonts** to use your theme's body and heading fonts with lighter weights, so the widget blends into your store.

## How it works

When a product has subscription plans configured:

1. The widget displays one-time purchase and subscription options
2. Customers choose a frequency, from a dropdown in Classic or buttons in Optimized
3. Each option shows its discount, and free shipping where the plan has it
4. When they add to cart, the subscription plan is attached to the cart item

Prepaid plans show the price per delivery and the total per charge; see [Prepaid and commitment plans](/docs/subscriptions/prepaid-plans).
