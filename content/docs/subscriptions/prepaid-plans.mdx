## Prepaid plans

A prepaid plan bills less often than it delivers: for example, deliver every month and bill every three months, so the customer pays for three deliveries up front.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    Go to **Subscriptions > Products**, open the product and add or edit a plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the delivery frequency">
    Choose how often the product ships, for example every month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the billing frequency">
    Under **Billing frequency (advanced)**, set a longer interval, for example every 3 months. Billing must be a whole multiple of delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the plan. Shopify creates the scheduled deliveries for each charge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the product page the subscribe box shows the price per delivery and the total per charge, and the frequency option reads, for example, **Every month · 3 deliveries prepaid**.

## Minimum number of orders

**Minimum billing cycles** is a commitment: the number of charges before the customer can cancel. The subscribe box shows it as **Minimum N orders**. Leave it at 1 for no commitment.
