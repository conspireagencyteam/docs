When a shopper writes to you in Gorgias, Bonde shows their subscriptions in the ticket sidebar. Your agents can act on them from there, and every action lands on the subscription's timeline in Bonde with the ticket number. If you use Gorgias AI Agent, it can run the same actions on its own within the permissions you set; see [Gorgias AI Agent](/docs/integrations/gorgias-ai-agent).

Gorgias is included in Bonde Pro ($15 a month flat, no transaction fees).

## Requirements

- Bonde Pro
- A Gorgias account, and a Gorgias user with admin access to connect it
- Your Shopify store connected in Gorgias, so tickets carry the shopper's email

The sidebar and its buttons work with the Gorgias Helpdesk. AI Agent actions also need Gorgias AI Agent on your account.

## Connect Gorgias

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Gorgias page in Bonde">
    Go to **Settings > Gorgias**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Gorgias account">
    Type your Gorgias subdomain, the part before `.gorgias.com` in your helpdesk address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect Gorgias**. Gorgias asks you to sign in and approve Bonde. You come back to Bonde when it is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Bonde set up your helpdesk">
    Bonde creates three things in your Gorgias account: an HTTP integration named **Bonde Subscriptions**, a ticket sidebar widget, and four macros. The status on the Gorgias page turns to **Connected** when it is done, usually within a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the action buttons">
    Bonde adds seven action buttons to every subscription in the sidebar when it sets up the widget, with the secret already in place. Open a ticket from a subscriber to see them. Nothing is added by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your secret for AI Agent">
    The sidebar and its buttons need nothing from you. If you build AI Agent actions or a custom integration, click **Rotate secret** on the **Bonde Connect secret** card; the secret is shown once, so keep it somewhere safe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Gorgias finds the shopper by the email on the ticket. The sidebar shows subscriptions only when that email matches the customer's email in Shopify.
</Note>

## What agents see

The sidebar shows one card per subscription the shopper has. Each card carries:

- Status (Active, Paused, Cancelled)
- The products, with quantities
- Frequency, and the price per order
- The next order date, or the resume date when paused
- The payment method, and whether the last payment failed
- The subscription's title opens it in Bonde inside your Shopify admin, and a **Customer in Shopify** link at the top of the card opens the customer

A shopper with no subscriptions gets no card, so the sidebar stays out of the way on ordinary tickets.

## The buttons

Bonde adds the buttons to the widget when it connects, so they are on every subscription card from the first ticket. Every button runs inside Gorgias and reports its result on the ticket: a confirmation when it worked, the reason when it did not. Where a button needs a value, Gorgias opens a small form and asks the agent for it before anything happens. The **Sidebar buttons** card in Bonde lists the same definitions, in case one is ever removed in the Gorgias widget editor and needs adding back.

| Button | What it does | Asks for |
|---|---|---|
| **Skip next order** | Moves the next order forward by one billing interval. The shopper gets the skip confirmation email. | Nothing |
| **Pause** | Pauses the subscription. Nothing bills while it is paused. | A resume date, optional |
| **Resume** | Resumes a paused subscription. A past due date is advanced before billing restarts. | Nothing |
| **Cancel** | Cancels the subscription permanently and records the reason. | A reason, required |
| **Reschedule** | Moves the next order to a chosen date, between tomorrow and a year ahead. | The date |
| **Apply discount** | Adds a percentage or fixed discount to the next order, or to a number of orders. | The type, the value, and how many orders |
| **Send payment update link** | Gives the shopper a secure link, or an email, to update their payment method. | Nothing |

A button that is turned off for support agents in [Permissions](#permissions) returns a short message on the ticket and changes nothing.

Each action appears on the subscription's timeline in Bonde (**Subscriptions > Contracts**, then the contract) as taken by support, with the Gorgias ticket number, so you can always see who did what and why.

## Macros

Bonde adds four macros to Gorgias, one for each of the most common replies:

- Next order skipped
- Subscription paused
- Subscription cancelled
- Payment update link sent

They fill in the shopper's subscription details from the sidebar data. Edit the wording in Gorgias under **Settings > Macros**, like any other macro.

## Permissions

The permissions table on **Settings > Gorgias** decides, for each action, whether support agents may run it and whether AI Agent may run it. Reading subscriptions is always allowed. Defaults:

| Action | Support agents | AI Agent |
|---|---|---|
| Skip the next order | Allowed | Allowed |
| Pause | Allowed | Allowed |
| Resume | Allowed | Allowed |
| Cancel | Allowed | Allowed |
| Change the next order date | Allowed | Allowed |
| Change the frequency | Allowed | Allowed |
| Swap a product | Allowed | Off |
| Update the shipping address | Allowed | Allowed |
| Apply a discount | Allowed | Off |
| Retry a failed payment | Allowed | Off |
| Send a payment update link | Allowed | Allowed |

Swapping, discounting and charging a card again are off for AI Agent until you switch them on. Cancelling is on, because shoppers ask for it; the AI recipe has it offer a pause or a skip first and confirm before it cancels, and you can switch it off here. Changes apply immediately. A store connected before 9 November 2026 keeps the permissions it had, with Cancel off for AI until you switch it on.

<Note>
These permissions are separate from the customer portal settings. You can let support reschedule orders without letting shoppers do it in the portal, or the reverse.
</Note>

## The Bonde Connect secret

The secret is what proves to Bonde that a request comes from your Gorgias account. It is shown once, when you connect, and Bonde keeps only a fingerprint of it afterwards.

Click **Rotate secret** on the **Bonde Connect secret** card to issue a new one. The old secret stops working at once, and the new one is shown once. Bonde updates the sidebar integration and its buttons itself. Any AI Agent action and any custom integration built with the old secret keep failing until you paste the new one into them; see [after rotating the secret](/docs/integrations/gorgias-ai-agent#after-rotating-the-secret).

<Warning>
Treat the secret like a password. Anyone who has it can read and change subscriptions on your store, within the permissions you set.
</Warning>

## Testing

Use the **Test** card on **Settings > Gorgias** to look up an email address the way Gorgias would. It shows exactly what the sidebar will render for that shopper, including the actions allowed, without opening a ticket. Try it with a subscriber's email after connecting, and again after changing permissions.

## Troubleshooting

**No widget in the sidebar.** Either the email on the ticket does not match the customer's email in Shopify, or the shopper has no subscriptions. Check the email in Shopify under **Customers**, then run the same email through the **Test** card in Bonde.

**"Needs attention" on the Gorgias page.** Something Bonde created in Gorgias is missing or has changed, usually because it was edited or deleted in Gorgias. Click **Repair**. Bonde recreates the integration, the widget with its buttons and the macros it manages, and leaves everything else alone.

**The card shows old details.** Gorgias refreshes the sidebar when something happens on the ticket (a new message, a tag, an action). After a change made elsewhere, reopen the ticket or add a tag to refresh it.

**"Reconnect required".** Gorgias no longer accepts Bonde's access, for example after the connecting user was removed or the app was uninstalled on the Gorgias side. Click **Connect Gorgias** again and approve Bonde. Your permissions are kept.

**A button returns an error.** The message on the ticket says why: the action is off for support agents, the subscription is not in a state that allows it (a cancelled subscription cannot be paused), or your store is no longer on Bonde Pro.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** on **Settings > Gorgias**. Bonde removes the integration, widget and macros it created in Gorgias and revokes the secret. Subscriptions, their timelines and your permission settings stay in Bonde. Uninstalling Bonde from Shopify does the same.
