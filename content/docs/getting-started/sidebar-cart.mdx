Bonde's sidebar cart (also called a cart drawer) replaces your theme's default cart with a feature-rich experience that includes upsell display, bundle grouping, and subscription information.

## Features

- **Upsell display**: Show cart upsells directly in the sidebar
- **Bundle grouping**: Bundle items appear grouped together with expand/collapse
- **Subscription display**: Shows delivery frequency for subscription items
- **Customizable styling**: Match your theme's look and feel
- **Smooth animations**: Modern slide-in/out behavior

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the app embed">
    Go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, click **App Embeds**, and enable the **Bonde** app embed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the sidebar cart">
    In the Bonde app, go to **Upsells > Settings > Cart sidebar** and turn on **Enable the sidebar cart**. This is the app setting: it applies to every theme whose Bonde app embed is set to **Use app setting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    Customize the sidebar cart appearance and behavior in the settings panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test it on an unpublished theme first

Each theme can decide on its own whether the sidebar cart shows, so you don't have to change your live store to try it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme's editor">
    Go to **Online Store > Themes**, find the unpublished theme you want to test on, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sidebar cart for that theme">
    Click **App embeds**, open **Bonde**, and set **Sidebar cart on this theme** to **On**. Make sure the Bonde app embed itself is switched on, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Preview the theme and add something to the cart to see the drawer. When you're happy, publish the theme: the setting travels with it, so the sidebar cart goes live at the same moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Sidebar cart on this theme** has three values. **Use app setting** (the default) follows the switch in the Bonde app. **On** and **Off** override it for that theme only. The **Where the sidebar cart shows** table in **Upsells > Settings > Cart sidebar** lists every theme with its current setting and a link to its editor.
</Note>

## Configuration options

In **Upsells > Settings > Cart sidebar**, you can configure:

- **Enable/disable**: Turn the sidebar cart on or off for every theme that uses the app setting
- **Styling**: Colors, fonts, and spacing to match your theme
- **Upsell display**: Whether to show upsells in the cart and how many
- **Bundle display**: How bundle items are grouped and displayed

## Cart upsells

When you create cart upsells in Bonde, they automatically appear in the sidebar cart. See [Cart upsells](/docs/upsells/cart) for details on creating upsell offers.

## Troubleshooting

**Sidebar cart not appearing?**
- Make sure the Bonde app embed is enabled in your theme
- Check that the sidebar cart is enabled in **Upsells > Settings > Cart sidebar**, and that the theme's Bonde app embed isn't set to **Off** under **Sidebar cart on this theme**
- Some themes have their own cart drawers that may conflict - you may need to disable the theme's cart drawer

**Cart upsells not showing?**
- Verify you have active cart upsells configured
- Check that the trigger conditions match the current cart contents
