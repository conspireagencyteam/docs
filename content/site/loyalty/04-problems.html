//WHY LOYALTY FALLS FLAT

Lack of flexibility is why customers leave.

Most loyalty apps maintain their own credit balance separate from Shopify. So at checkout, the customer has to copy a code, paste it in a discount field, and hope it works. Half don't bother. The other half churn through the friction.

01

Codes at checkout kill redemption.

Customers earn 'points' in one system, redeem with a discount code in another. Half forget. Half mistype. The reward they earned doesn't apply, and your most engaged customer just had their worst checkout experience.

02

B2B and multi-currency don't work.

Legacy loyalty apps assume one currency and one customer type. Wholesale customers get nothing. International customers fight with FX. Programs that should expand revenue actively shrink your eligible base.

03

Liability lives in a black box.

When credit lives outside Shopify, your finance team can't reconcile it, expirations drift out of sync with refunds, and you can never quite trust the balance. Loyalty turns into a liability you can't quantify.
