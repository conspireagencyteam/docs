CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Will my customers need to enter a code at checkout?

    Never. Bonde uses Shopify's native store credit, so when a loyalty member reaches checkout, their balance is already there and applies automatically. There's nothing for the customer to do — which is why redemption rates jump from ~35% industry-standard to nearly 100%.

  • Can I exclude B2B customers from earning rewards?

    Yes — Bonde gives DTC and B2B totally separate program controls. You can exclude B2B entirely, give them a reduced reward rate, or run a separate B2B-only program with its own rules. Programs respect audience boundaries automatically.

  • Does Bonde Loyalty support multiple currencies?

    Yes. Because credit lives in Shopify's native store credit ledger, multi-currency support is built in. Customers earn and redeem in their local currency — no FX math, no broken redemptions.

  • Can I migrate from Smile, Loyalty Lion, or Yotpo?

    There's no self-serve importer for other loyalty apps yet. Store credit you've already issued in Shopify stays with your customers when you switch, because Bonde pays rewards into the same native store credit. For points held inside another app, email us before you switch and we'll work out the move with you.

  • What happens to store credit customers already have?

    Nothing. It lives in Shopify's native store credit, not in Bonde, so it keeps working at checkout and new Bonde rewards add to the same balance.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No contracts. Monthly billing, cancel anytime from your Shopify admin.
