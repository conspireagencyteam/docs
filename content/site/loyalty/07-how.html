//HOW IT WORKS

Loyalty live by lunch.

  1. 01

    Install the app

    One click from the Shopify App Store. Bonde connects directly to Shopify's native store credit — no separate balance system to set up.

  2. 02

    Build your first program

    Pick a trigger (any order, specific products, subscription renewal, etc.). Set the reward (% of order or fixed credit). Choose B2B or DTC. Save as Draft, Active, or Paused.

  3. 03

    Ship it and watch redemption climb

    Activate the program. Credits start accruing on every qualifying order, redeem automatically at checkout, and show up in the analytics dashboard. Reconciled in Shopify admin from day one.
