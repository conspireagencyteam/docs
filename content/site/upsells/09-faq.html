CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Will upsells hurt my conversion rate?

    No — Bonde Upsells is tuned to add to intent, not interrupt it. In-context placements (not pop-ups), smart frequency capping, and opt-in offer types. Most stores see AOV lift within 30 days with no change in conversion rate.

  • Can I run different offers on different surfaces?

    Yes. Every surface has its own offer library, and you can target by cart contents, order history, discount code, or customer tag. One product can power completely different offers across PDP, cart, checkout, and post-purchase.

  • Does checkout upsell work on Shopify Basic?

    Checkout upsells require Shopify Plus (they run on Checkout Extensibility). PDP, sidebar cart, cart page, post-purchase, and tracking-page upsells all work on every Shopify plan.

  • How fast do I see results?

    Most stores see measurable AOV lift within 48 hours of shipping their first upsell. Full conversion-rate and accept-rate data is in the dashboard from day one.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No contracts. Monthly billing, cancel anytime from your Shopify admin.
