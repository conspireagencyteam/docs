//WHY MOST STORES STRUGGLE

You've probably tried bundles before. Here's why they didn't work.

Most bundle apps are duct-taped together — built for a single use case, buggy at scale, and invisible to the people who actually need to see them: your customers.

01

They only support one bundle type.

Fixed bundles, mix-and-match, build-a-box, tiered discounts — most apps pick one and call it done. Your catalog deserves better.

02

They break your theme.

Clunky custom code that fights your Shopify theme. Slow load times, broken checkout, designers pulling their hair out.

03

They live in a silo.

A bundle app that doesn't talk to your cart, subscriptions, or loyalty program is just a discount engine. That's not growth.
