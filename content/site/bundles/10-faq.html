CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Will bundles slow down my Shopify store?

    No. Bonde uses Shopify's native app embed system and lazy-loads all bundle UI. Typical impact: under 30ms on mobile. We publish real Core Web Vitals data in the docs.

  • Can I run bundles with my existing discount codes?

    Yes. Bonde Bundles stack cleanly with Shopify's native discount codes so you can run site-wide promos alongside bundle offers.

  • Does Bonde Bundles work with Shopify Subscriptions?

    Yes. Bundles can be configured as one-time or recurring. Shoppers can choose their cadence at checkout and bundle pricing applies to each shipment.

  • What happens to inventory when someone buys a bundle?

    Inventory is drawn from the individual SKUs you set in the bundle — no parent SKU, no inventory drift. Fulfillment stays native.

  • Can I migrate from another bundles app?

    Yes — migration is free. Our team imports your existing bundles and helps you cut over with zero downtime.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No contracts. Monthly billing, cancel anytime from your Shopify admin.
