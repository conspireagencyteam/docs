//HOW IT WORKS

Set up subscriptions in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install Bonde

    Install from the Shopify App Store and start the 14-day free trial of Bonde Pro.

  2. 02

    Add plans to your products

    Pick the delivery frequencies, set a discount for each plan and turn on free shipping where you want it.

  3. 03

    Add the widgets

    Add the subscription block to your product page and the subscriptions page to customer accounts. Bonde's setup guide opens the theme editor at the right spot.
