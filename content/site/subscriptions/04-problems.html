//WHY SUBSCRIBERS LEAVE

Lack of flexibility is why customers leave.

Your subscribers don't want to cancel. They want to skip an order. Change their shipping address. Swap a flavor. But when the only option is 'cancel or email support,' they pick cancel — and they don't come back.

01

Cancel is easier than flexibility.

Customers want to skip one order because they have too much product. No self-service button exists. Their easiest path is canceling — and 60% never come back.

02

Nothing rewards loyalty.

Subscribers get treated the same as one-time buyers. No store credit, no VIP tier, no exclusive perks. Where's the upside for committing to recurring?

03

No one's fighting to keep them.

The cancel button has zero intelligence. No save offer. No pause option. No last-chance discount. Your subscription app watches them leave and does nothing.
