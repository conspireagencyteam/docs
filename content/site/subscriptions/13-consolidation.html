//ORDER CONSOLIDATION

Two subscriptions, one package.

When a customer has subscriptions billing a few days apart, Bonde spots it and lines them up so they ship together. You pay for one shipment instead of two.

  • Finds subscriptions billing within a window you set, from 1 to 14 days
  • Shows the shipping you'd save, then aligns the dates in one click
  • Optionally lets customers line up their own from the portal
  • Combines renewals that fall on the same day automatically
