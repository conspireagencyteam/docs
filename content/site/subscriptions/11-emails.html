//LIFECYCLE EMAILS

The emails around every renewal, from your own domain.

An upcoming-charge notice a few days before each renewal, a confirmation when it goes through and a heads-up when the card is about to expire. Each one is a template you edit, sent from your verified domain through Bonde.

  • Upcoming charge, with the lead time you choose
  • Charge confirmation, skip confirmation and cancellation
  • Card expiring, before the renewal it would break
  • Resuming soon, for paused subscriptions with an end date

Templates stay off until you activate them, so nothing goes out before you have reviewed it.
