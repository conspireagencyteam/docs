//LOYALTY FOR SUBSCRIBERS

Reward subscribers for staying subscribed.

Bonde Loyalty pays store credit when someone subscribes and again on every renewal, as a fixed amount or a percentage of the order. It's native Shopify store credit, so customers see their balance in their account and spend it at checkout.

  • Credit for starting a subscription, and a bonus on a milestone renewal
  • Credit on every renewal, fixed or a percentage; win-back credit for a subscriber who comes back
  • Spend it on renewals: the balance is applied to the next charge automatically
  • Limit it to products or collections, with expiry dates and caps, plus an email each time a customer earns
