CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Does Bonde use Shopify's own subscription system?

    Yes. Bonde is built on Shopify's native subscription contracts and selling plans, so payments, taxes and checkout stay in Shopify. There's no separate billing system to run.

  • How much do subscriptions cost?

    Subscriptions are part of Bonde Pro: $15 a month, with no transaction fees and no percentage of your subscription revenue, however many subscribers you have. Pro starts with a 14-day free trial.

  • Can customers change their subscription without contacting me?

    Yes. From Shopify customer accounts they can skip an order, swap products, change the delivery frequency, pause and resume, update their card and cancel.

  • What happens when a renewal payment fails?

    Bonde emails the customer a link to update their card and retries the charge 3, 7, 14 and 21 days after the failure, then every 28 days. Failures a retry can't fix, like a revoked card, aren't retried.

  • Can I move my subscribers from another app?

    Yes. Bonde imports subscriptions from a CSV export (Recharge, Bold, WooCommerce or your own format), including saved payment methods from Stripe, Braintree, Authorize.net and PayPal. Contact us and we'll run the move with you.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No. Bonde bills monthly through Shopify, and you can cancel any time from your Shopify admin.
