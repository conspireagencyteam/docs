//COMMON QUESTIONS

Things merchants ask before installing.

Still have questions? Our team usually replies within a few hours.

CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Does Bonde use Shopify's own subscription system?

    Yes. Bonde is built on Shopify's native subscription contracts and selling plans, so payments, taxes and checkout stay in Shopify. There's no separate billing system to run.

  • How much do subscriptions cost?

    Subscriptions are part of Bonde Pro: $15 a month, with no transaction fees and no percentage of your subscription revenue, however many subscribers you have. Pro starts with a 14-day free trial.

  • Can customers change their subscription without contacting me?

    Yes. From Shopify customer accounts they can skip an order or pick a new date, order now, swap products, change the delivery frequency, quantity or address, add a one-time product to the next order, pause for a set time or until they come back, update their card, restart a cancelled subscription and cancel. Turn each action on or off in Bonde.

  • What happens when a renewal payment fails?

    Bonde emails the customer a link to update their card and retries the charge on your schedule, by default 3, 7, 14 and 21 days after the failure. After the last attempt the subscription is paused until the card is updated (or cancelled, if you choose that). Failures a retry can't fix, like a revoked card, aren't retried.

  • Can I move my subscribers from another app?

    Yes. Bonde imports subscriptions from a CSV export (Recharge, Skio, Bold, Shopify Subscriptions, WooCommerce or your own format), including saved payment methods from Stripe, Braintree, Authorize.net and PayPal. The importer is in the app under Subscriptions. Contact us and we'll run the move with you.

  • Can I offer a lower price on the first order?

    Yes. Each plan can have an introductory offer: a bigger discount, a fixed amount off or a set price for the first order (or the first few orders), then the ongoing subscriber discount. The subscribe box shows both prices, the cart says "30% off first order, then save 15%", and Bonde moves the price to the ongoing discount when the introductory orders are done.

  • Can I give a free gift with the first order, then perks on later ones?

    That is a subscription journey. Pick the gift, and the cart offers it as soon as a subscription is added, free only while the subscription stays in the cart. Then add what each later order gets: free shipping, a discount or a free product on the renewal you choose. Bonde applies each perk right before that order is charged and shows the timeline in the subscribe box, in the customer's account and in the upcoming-order email.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No. Bonde bills monthly through Shopify, and you can cancel any time from your Shopify admin. Your own plans can have one, though: set a minimum number of orders before a subscriber can cancel, or a prepaid plan that bills several deliveries at once.
