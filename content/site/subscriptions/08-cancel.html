//SAVE THE CANCEL

Ask why before they cancel, then offer a way to stay.

When a customer taps cancel, Bonde asks for the reason and shows one save offer matched to it: skip the next order, pause for a while, swap the product, change the frequency, take a discount on the next order or get a free gift in it. You choose which offers go with which reason.

  • Reasons and offers you set, shown in your customer account
  • The offer runs itself: the skip, pause, discount or free gift is applied on the spot
  • Cancellation reasons and save rate in your Churn analytics
  • Pause for one, two or three months and the subscription resumes by itself, with a reminder email first
