//FAILED PAYMENTS

Declined cards get retried, not forgotten.

When a renewal charge is declined, Bonde emails the customer a link to update their card and retries the charge on a schedule you set, 3, 7, 14 and 21 days after the failure by default. After the last attempt the subscription is paused until the card is updated, or cancelled if you prefer.

  • A card-declined email from your own domain, linking to the payment update
  • Your own retry schedule and number of attempts, then pause or cancel
  • Failures a retry can't fix, like a revoked card, aren't retried
  • A daily check catches any renewal that didn't run

No email domain set up yet? Shopify sends its own update-payment email instead.
