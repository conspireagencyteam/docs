//SUBSCRIPTION JOURNEYS

Front-load the first orders so people stay for the third.

Most subscribers who leave do it around the second or third order. A journey gives them a reason to get there: an introductory price on the first order, a free gift at checkout, free shipping on order two, a discount on order three. You set it up once; Bonde applies each perk to the right order and shows customers what is coming.

  • Introductory pricing on any plan: a bigger discount for the first order or first few orders, then the ongoing discount
  • A free gift the customer claims in the cart when they add a subscription
  • Discounts, free shipping or a free product on the renewal you choose, applied before the order is charged
  • The timeline shows in the subscribe box, in the customer's account and in the upcoming-order email
  • Store credit from Bonde Loyalty appears on the same timeline, so you see the whole experience in one place
