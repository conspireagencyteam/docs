//BUILD-A-BOX SUBSCRIPTIONS

Let customers subscribe to a box they built.

Build-a-box bundles get a subscribe-and-save option as soon as the bundle has plans. The whole box renews on schedule, and it stays grouped as one item in the cart and at checkout.

  • Customers choose what goes in the box and how often it arrives
  • Grouped in the cart with its delivery schedule
  • Skip, pause or swap items in the box like any other subscription
