CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Will Bonde work with existing Shopify Subscriptions?

    Yes. Bonde runs on Shopify's native subscription infrastructure — no separate billing system, no duplicate data. Moving from Recharge, Skio or Appstle? Connect your old app with an API key and Bonde reads your active subscribers, then recreates each plan, schedule and next charge date after you review them. Subscribers whose card is already saved in Shopify keep it. Other apps can import with a CSV. See the migration guide.

  • How does loyalty integration work?

    Subscribers earn store credit on every recurring order automatically. Credit stacks with VIP tiers and applies at checkout or on the customer's next delivery. No separate loyalty app required.

  • Can customers skip orders without contacting support?

    Yes — the self-service portal lets customers skip, swap, pause, change cadence, and update shipping or payment on their own. Most stores see support tickets drop 70% within a month.

  • Do I need a separate billing system?

    No. Bonde runs on Shopify's native subscription billing, so payments, taxes, and checkout stay inside Shopify. No third-party billing to manage.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No contracts. Monthly billing, cancel anytime from your Shopify admin.
