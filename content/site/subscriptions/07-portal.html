//CUSTOMER PORTAL

A subscription portal inside Shopify customer accounts.

Customers manage their subscription where they already check their orders, without emailing you. Each change takes a couple of taps, and every one of them is an alternative to cancelling.

  • Skip an order, pick a new date or order now
  • Swap to another variant or product, keeping the subscriber price
  • Change how often it arrives, the quantity or the address
  • Pause for a while and resume by themselves, or on the date they chose
  • Add a one-time product to the next order, update the card, restart a cancelled subscription
