//CUSTOMER PORTAL

A subscription portal inside Shopify customer accounts.

Customers manage their subscription where they already check their orders, without emailing you. Each change takes a couple of taps, and every one of them is an alternative to cancelling.

  • Skip an order, with the skip lengths you choose
  • Swap to another variant or product
  • Change how often it arrives
  • Pause, and resume whenever they're ready
  • Update the card on file
