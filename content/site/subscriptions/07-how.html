//HOW IT WORKS

Subscriptions live in an afternoon.

  1. 01

    Install the app

    One click from the Shopify App Store. Bonde installs subscription templates across your theme automatically.

  2. 02

    Pick subscription-eligible products

    Mark products as subscription-eligible, set discount tiers, configure delivery cadences. Turn on Loyalty integration with one toggle.

  3. 03

    Publish and ship

    Publish. Subscribers start managing their own deliveries. Retention lift shows up in the dashboard within 30 days.
