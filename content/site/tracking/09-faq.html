Things merchants ask before installing.

CONTACT SUPPORT
  • Will this replace my Shopify order status page?

    Yes — your order confirmation emails route to your new branded page at store.com/track. The default Shopify status page still works, but customers land on yours.

  • Which carriers are supported?

    USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, plus 900+ regional and international carriers. If your 3PL ships it, we track it.

  • Will it work with my 3PL and fulfillment provider?

    Yes. Bonde ingests tracking data from Shopify's order feed, so anything your fulfillment provider already reports to Shopify flows through automatically.

  • Do I need to migrate from another tracking app?

    No — you can run both side-by-side during the switchover. When you're ready, update your order confirmation email templates to point at the Bonde URL.

  • How do SMS updates work — do I need a separate provider?

    SMS is included on Growth and Plus plans. Bonde handles opt-in collection and sends updates under your brand — no separate SMS provider needed.

  • Is there a contract or minimum term?

    No contracts. Monthly billing, cancel anytime from your Shopify admin.
