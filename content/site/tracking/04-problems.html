//WHY MOST TRACKING IS BROKEN

Your tracking page is your competitors' billboard.

When customers leave your site to check an order, they land on a UPS or USPS page with your competitors' ads running next to your shipment. You paid for that traffic. Someone else is monetizing it.

01

Carriers own your most engaged moment.

FedEx, UPS, USPS — they'll happily host your customer while showing ads for their other partners. That's your audience, on their platform.

02

Shopify's default page is a dead end.

The built-in order status page is unbranded, ugly, and has zero upsell or retention mechanisms. Great for developers, worthless for growth.

03

Support tickets pile up anyway.

Without proactive updates, customers email support. Every WISMO ticket costs money, support time, and brand goodwill you won't get back.
